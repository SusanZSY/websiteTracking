Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to help you feel confident.

What is TMatch?

TMatch is a client-facing therapist matching platform that helps you discover care with the guidance of Tracy, an AI case manager.

Is TMatch free to use?

Yes. It is free for clients to talk with Tracy, review therapist matches, and browse provider profiles.

Is Tracy an AI or a real person?

Tracy is an AI case manager designed to understand your needs and help identify therapists who fit.

Does TMatch accept insurance?

TMatch helps you narrow matches based on insurance preference, but coverage is confirmed directly with the therapist.
